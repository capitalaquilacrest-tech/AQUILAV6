Why Aquila

A connected platform, not just a login page.

Aquila combines account tools, dashboard monitoring, Capital Reserve features, transaction visibility, Telegram updates, Discord resources, and support into one member experience.

Aquila Crest Capital was created to make the member experience clearer, more organized, and easier to access. The platform brings together the established Aquila system, member records, account activity, capital options, official announcements, and support channels in one connected digital environment.

Our focus is not to replace the system our members already know. It is to strengthen it through better presentation, improved accessibility, clearer records, and a consistent mobile-first experience. Existing platform rules, member processes, and operational workflows remain aligned with the current Aquila structure.

More than a website, Aquila is a growing community built around organization, communication, and continuous improvement. As the platform develops, every enhancement is designed to support the same trusted member journey while making the experience more professional, responsive, and ready for the future.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers before you continue.

30 questions available
What is Aquila Crest Capital?

Aquila Crest Capital is a digital member platform that centralizes account tools, transaction monitoring, Capital Reserve features, community access, and support resources.

Can I use the platform on mobile?

Yes. The landing page and member dashboard are designed for phones, tablets, and desktop browsers.

How do I see my activity?

Members can review deposit, withdrawal, reinvest, income, bonus, support, and Capital Reserve records inside the dashboard.

What is Passive Yield?

Passive Yield is 3% daily based on the member’s actual total investment over a fixed 60-day term.

What is the minimum Passive Yield investment?

The minimum investment is ₱1,000.

How long is the Passive Yield term?

The fixed term is 60 days.

What is Direct Yield?

Direct Yield is a 5% bonus on every qualified investment made by a directly invited member.

What is Network Yield?

Network Yield is 5% based on daily earnings generated by a qualified network, up to Level 5 only.

What is the Welcome Bonus?

The Welcome Bonus applies to the first deposit only. Its rate ranges from 5% to 15%, depending on the first deposit amount, and is credited to the investment before the 3% daily return is calculated.

What is the Reload Bonus?

The Reload Bonus is 5% on qualified deposits from the 2nd through the 15th deposit. It is credited to the investment before the 3% daily return is calculated and no longer applies from the 16th deposit onward.

Can I reinvest?

Eligible members may submit reinvestment requests according to the schedule and rules displayed in the member portal.

Where can I track my earnings?

Passive, Direct, Network, bonus, reinvestment, and transaction histories are available inside the member dashboard.

What is Capital Reserve?

Capital Reserve allows eligible members to choose a reserve amount and an eligible maturity period, then review the projected growth before submission.

How does Capital Reserve work?

The calculator applies the minimum maturity required for the entered amount. Members may choose an eligible later date up to the platform maximum and review the projection before proceeding.

What is the minimum Capital Reserve amount?

The minimum Capital Reserve amount is ₱300.

What is the Capital Reserve maturity range?

Eligible maturity selections range from 7 days up to 365 days, subject to the required minimum for the reserve amount.

What is the minimum maturity for ₱300–₱4,999?

The minimum maturity is 7 days.

What is the minimum maturity for ₱5,000–₱9,999?

The minimum maturity is 15 days.

What is the minimum maturity for ₱10,000–₱24,999?

The minimum maturity is 30 days.

What is the minimum maturity for ₱25,000–₱49,999?

The minimum maturity is 45 days.

What is the minimum maturity for ₱50,000–₱99,999?

The minimum maturity is 60 days.

What is the minimum maturity for ₱100,000–₱249,999?

The minimum maturity is 90 days.

What is the minimum maturity for ₱250,000 and above?

The minimum maturity is 120 days.

Can I choose a later Capital Reserve maturity?

Yes. You may choose any eligible later maturity up to 365 days.

When does a Capital Reserve become active?

It becomes active after payment review and approval.

Where can I track my Capital Reserve?

Active contracts, projections, remaining days, maturity, and status are available inside the member dashboard.

Are the activity cards updated automatically?

Yes. Public-safe approved deposits, released withdrawals, active Capital Reserves, and approved reinvestments are loaded automatically.

Are withdrawal photos updated automatically?

Yes. New images added to the official payout folder can appear automatically in the withdrawal gallery.

How do I access the Member Portal?

Select LOGIN DASHBOARD anywhere on this website. New members may also use CREATE ACCOUNT to begin.

Where do members get help?

Support is available through the member dashboard and organized community channels.
