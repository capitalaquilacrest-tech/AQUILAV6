Privacy Policy

How Aquila handles member and website information.

Effective date: July 30, 2026. This policy applies to the official Aquila Crest Capital website and member-facing digital services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information submitted during registration, identity or account verification, deposits, withdrawals, reinvestments, Capital Reserve activity, support requests, and use of the Member Portal. This may include account identifiers, contact details, transaction references, payment details, verification records, device or browser information, and communication history.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to operate member accounts, process and review platform requests, maintain transaction records, prevent duplicate or unauthorized activity, provide support, deliver official notifications, improve platform performance, comply with applicable obligations, and protect the integrity of the Aquila system.

3. Google and Third-Party Services

The platform may use Google Apps Script, Google Sheets, payment gateways, Telegram, Discord, Facebook Messenger, email, analytics, hosting providers, and other service providers. These services may process information under their own privacy terms. Aquila shares only information reasonably required for the relevant service or transaction.

4. Public-Safe Display

The public landing page will not intentionally display passwords, complete payment account numbers, government identification images, or other sensitive member records. Any public statistics or activity previews should be aggregated, anonymized, or specifically approved for public display.

5. Security and Account Responsibility

Reasonable technical and operational safeguards are used to protect records. No online system can guarantee absolute security. Members must protect their passwords, devices, OTPs, and account access, and must immediately report suspected unauthorized activity.

6. Retention and Corrections

Records may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for platform operations, dispute handling, accounting, security, and legal or regulatory purposes. Members may request correction of inaccurate personal information by contacting the official support email, subject to identity verification and record-retention requirements.

7. Communications

By joining official Aquila channels, members may receive platform announcements, transaction notices, maintenance advisories, policy changes, and support communications. Community channels should not be used to send passwords, OTPs, or complete sensitive financial information.

8. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the platform, service providers, or operational requirements change. The latest published version on the official website will apply from its stated effective date.

Questions may be sent to capitalaquilacrest@gmail.com.
